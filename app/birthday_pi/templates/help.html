Help

You do everything on your phone. The screen is just for looking at.

  1. A reminder is on the screen

    Buy the gift, send the card, or make the call. Then tap Got it on your home page. The screen clears by itself.

  2. Family sent photos

    Your home page says "2 photos need your OK". Tap Review, then Yes, show it or No. Only photos you say yes to go on the screen.

  3. It asks "Is this …?" about a face

    Sometimes {{ product }} isn't sure who is in a new photo. Tap Yes, No, or Not sure, skip. Any answer is fine, and you can't break anything.

  4. The screen is dim at night

    {% if night.on %}

    Your screen goes dark on purpose from {{ hour_label(night.start) }} until {{ hour_label(night.end) }}. A new reminder brightens it straight away.

    {% else %}

    Night dimming is off, so your screen stays bright all night.

    {% endif %} Change night hours

  5. The screen is blank or stuck

    Unplug the power from the small {{ product }} box (not the screen). Count to 10, plug it back in, and give it 2 minutes.
