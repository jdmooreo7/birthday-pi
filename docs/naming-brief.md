# Naming brief

**Status:** Started 2026-10-16 · **Decide by:** v1.0 (M10), before families we don't know install it · **Owner:** Product Manager; the Product Owner chooses

"Birthday Pi" was a working name. The product is now more than birthdays (anniversaries, family photos, faces), and a name with "Pi" in it ties us to one brand of hardware. The name people see is kept in one place in the code (`PRODUCT` in `app/birthday_pi/__init__.py`), so changing what families see is a one-line change. The repo, containers and the `birthdaypi` hostname can follow later.

## What it is, in one sentence
A screen in Grandma's home that keeps the family's birthdays, anniversaries and photos in front of her, run by a small box in the house, with nothing in the cloud.

## Who says the name
- **Grandma**, out loud, to a grandchild on the phone: "It's on the ___." It should sound like part of the home, not a gadget.
- **The family member who sets it up**, searching for it and telling a cousin about it.

## What the name must do
1. Easy to say and spell when heard once. Two syllables is ideal, three at most.
2. Warm and about family or home. No tech words (Pi, Hub, Smart, AI, Cloud, OS).
3. Not only about birthdays.
4. Available: no clash with a trademark in our area, a domain we can get, and a GitHub name.
5. Safe on the hardware: check the Raspberry Pi trademark rules before keeping anything with "Pi" in it.

## Directions to explore (none checked yet for trademarks or domains)
| Direction | Idea behind it | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| The place in the house | It lives where the family photos already are | Mantel, Mantelpiece, Hearthside |
| Keeping and remembering | It remembers so she doesn't have to | Keepsake, Rememberly, Keepdate |
| The family | It's about her people | Kinfolk Screen, Kinly, Our Lot |
| The moment | A reminder at the right time | Right On Time, Fond |

## How we decide
1. **Product Manager:** a longlist of about 20 names, each checked against the five rules above.
2. **Product Owner:** picks 3 to 5.
3. **Security Analyst:** trademark and domain checks on those.
4. **Designer:** tries each on the card, the phone pages and the welcome screen.
5. **During the pilot:** we listen for what Grandma and her family call it unprompted. That's the strongest signal we'll get.
6. **Product Owner decides by M10.** Then we rename what families see first; the repo and hostname follow in their own change.
